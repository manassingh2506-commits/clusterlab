Interactive web demo, personal project

ClusterLab

ClusterLab is an interactive playground for k-means clustering. You add data points, choose how many groups to look for, and watch the algorithm move its centres step by step until it finds the best grouping. It is the demo at the top of this page.

ProblemClustering is easy to describe but hard to picture; most explanations show only the final result.ResultA visual tool that shows every step, so learners can see why it works and when it fails.

Try the live demo

JavaScriptHTML Canvask-means
How it works

The algorithm starts by placing k centres. Each point joins the group of its nearest centre, then each centre moves to the average position of its group. These two steps repeat until no point changes group. I used the k-means++ method to choose starting centres that are spread apart, which gives better results.

Machine-learning project, personal

CricketIQ

CricketIQ predicts the chance that the chasing team will win a T20 match, updated after every ball. It learned from more than 900 past IPL matches and shows not just a number but the factors behind it, such as the required run rate and wickets in hand.

ProblemTV win predictors give a number without explaining why, so viewers cannot judge whether to trust it.ResultThe model picks the correct winner in about 78% of test matches from the halfway point of the chase.
Pythonpandasscikit-learnStreamlit
How it works

I converted ball-by-ball records into match situations: runs needed, balls left, wickets in hand, venue and toss result. A logistic regression model learns how each factor affects the chance of winning. I chose it because its results are easy to explain. I trained on older seasons and tested on the most recent ones, so the model never “saw the future”.

Time-series forecasting, personal project

AirAware

AirAware forecasts the Air Quality Index for Indian cities up to three days ahead, using past pollution levels and weather data. It shows the forecast with a shaded range, so users can see how certain the prediction is, and warns when the air is likely to become unhealthy.

ProblemMost air-quality apps only show today’s reading, which is too late for planning outdoor activities.ResultNext-day forecasts are usually within about 15% of the actual reading in testing.
PythonpandasXGBoostPlotly
How it works

I combined public pollution records with weather data such as wind speed, temperature and humidity. For each day, the model uses the previous week’s readings and the weather forecast. XGBoost learned the patterns; for example, low wind speed in winter usually means pollution builds up. The shaded range comes from two extra models that predict a high and a low estimate.

Natural language processing, personal project

SentiMeter

SentiMeter reads thousands of online product reviews and sorts them into positive, neutral and negative. It also finds which features people talk about most, such as battery or camera, so a buyer can understand the overall opinion in seconds.

ProblemPopular products have thousands of reviews, and star ratings alone hide what people actually like or dislike.ResultAbout 86% accuracy on a test set of reviews the model had not seen before.
PythonNLTKTF-IDFFlask
How it works

Each review is cleaned and converted into numbers using TF-IDF, which gives more weight to words that are important in a review but rare across all reviews. A logistic regression classifier then predicts the sentiment. To find topics, the program counts which product features appear most often near positive or negative words.
